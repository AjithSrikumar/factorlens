What is Passive Investing?

Passive investing means buying and holding a basket of securities that mirrors an index — without trying to pick individual winners or time the market.

Research consistently shows that over long periods, most active funds fail to beat their benchmarks after fees. Index funds give you low-cost, diversified exposure to proven return drivers.

80%+ of active large-cap funds underperform benchmarks over 10 years
Expense ratios: Active funds 1.5–2.5% vs Index funds 0.1–0.5%
No manager risk, no style drift, no surprises

What is Factor Investing?

Factor investing selects stocks based on specific characteristics that academic research has linked to higher long-term returns. These factors are systematic, rules-based, and emotion-free.

MomentumStocks that have recently risen tend to keep rising
QualityHigh ROE, low debt, stable earnings companies
Low VolLess volatile stocks with better risk-adjusted returns
ValueUndervalued companies with strong fundamentals
AlphaStocks with high excess returns vs market beta
Multi-FactorBlend of multiple factors for diversified exposure

Why Diversification Matters

No single factor wins every year. Momentum crushes in bull markets but crashes during reversals. By combining factors you reduce concentration risk and lower worst-case drawdown.
